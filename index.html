About the program

  • Started in 2015 and now runs from Pre-K through 5th grade.
  • Recognized by the New Jersey Department of Education as a Model Program for Spanish Dual Language Immersion.
  • Students learn about half of their core subjects in Spanish and half in English.
  • Open to all incoming Princeton kindergartners. This year Community Park has three full DLI kindergarten classes.
  • The pathway continues at Princeton Middle School and Princeton High School. The first cohort entered PHS in September 2023 and graduates in 2027.

Sources: PPS DLI page · Community Park School · CP World Languages · The Tower (PHS), Nov 2023

Research: Steele et al. (RAND), American Educational Research Journal (2017) · Umansky & Reardon, American Educational Research Journal (2014)
