Community Park DLI · Parent Action

Keep Dual Language Immersion at Community Park

What we know

  • Community Park teachers have asked for improvements to the existing DLI program.
  • The Superintendent is developing recommendations on the future of the DLI program, which he will present to the Board of Education on October 27.
  • A phase-out or elimination of the DLI program is being strongly considered.
  • The Board is expected to vote on the proposal at a later meeting.

Way 1 · Show up

Add the meetings to your calendar, then show up

Click Google or Apple / Outlook under each date to add it to your calendar with the time and place already filled in. A full room tells the Board how many families care, so come to as many as you can and make sure to speak up.

Mark your calendar

  • Oct18
    Meet the school board candidates, Sunday 3:00–4:00 PM, clocktower picnic area, Princeton Shopping Center. Ask Beth Behrend, Eleanor Hubbard and Adam Bierman where they stand on DLI.
  • Oct27
    Board of Education meeting, 7:00 PM. Location to be confirmed: 25 Valley Road or Princeton High School. Come and speak during public comment.
  • Oct29
    Parent forum with the superintendent, 8:30 AM, at Community Park School.
